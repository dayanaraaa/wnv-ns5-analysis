# Figures

This directory contains visualizations generated during an undergraduate research project investigating promising antiviral targets within the NS5 protein of West Nile Virus.

## Organization

### phylogeny
Phylogenetic trees and evolutionary relationships among flaviviruses.

### msa
Multiple sequence alignment visualizations and supporting files.

### whole_protein
Global NS5 protein analyses, including:
- AlphaFold structural models
- pLDDT confidence scores
- evolutionary-rate mappings
- disorder predictions

### candidate_pockets
Initial candidate drug pockets identified during exploratory analysis.

### proposed_targets
The final proposed antiviral targets (Pocket 0 and Pocket 4), selected based on:
- low evolutionary rates,
- high structural confidence,
- low intrinsic disorder,
- favorable accessibility.

These pockets represent the most promising candidates identified in this project.
